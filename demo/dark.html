The dark ground and the dark red

Catppuccin Mocha is out. Its base is #1E1E2E, which is a blue-purple and not a grey, and its red is #F38BA8, which is a pastel pink. What replaces them is a neutral macOS-like dark grey and a serious red that still sounds like the light theme’s crimson. Two questions, eight slices, one set of words in all of them — read the slices, ignore the copy.

The constraint that shapes every candidate here: a fully saturated crimson cannot reach 4.5:1 on a dark grey. The light theme’s own #7F0002 measures against #1E1E1E, and #AE002E only reaches .
